Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how information may be handled when you use this independent roofing information website or telephone the number displayed here. It also explains the website's lead-generation role and how inquiries may be referred to independent service providers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit this website or contact the telephone number displayed on it. The website is independently operated and provides information about roofing services. It is intended to help visitors make contact with relevant independent service providers; the website operator does not necessarily provide roofing services itself.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website and telephone service, information that may be collected includes information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, property location, and details of the roofing issue you describe. Telephone systems may also make a caller's telephone number or caller identification information available.

Information given during a telephone inquiry may include details about the service you are seeking and other information relevant to your request. Basic technical information may also be processed when the website is viewed, such as the date and time of a visit, the pages requested, browser type, device information, and IP address, depending on the website hosting arrangements.

The current website page does not contain a visitor form or embedded analytics script. No particular analytics provider or advertising tracking technology is identified here. The website operator should confirm the hosting provider's log practices and any telephone service configuration before publication.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to an inquiry, help connect a caller with a relevant service provider, facilitate a requested service discussion or consultation, maintain and improve the website and the quality of inquiries, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. Information should only be used in ways relevant to the inquiry and the website's stated purpose.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This website operates for informational and lead-generation purposes. When you call the telephone number displayed on the website, your call may be connected or referred to an independent third-party business that provides the roofing services you are asking about. The website operator may receive compensation for generating or referring qualifying inquiries.

Any service provider you are referred to is independent of this website operator. That provider is responsible for its own services, communications, customer relationship, and privacy practices. You should review the provider's privacy information and ask it how it will use your details before deciding whether to proceed.

Calling the displayed number does not, by itself, mean that you have agreed to receive unrelated marketing communications. Any further use of your information by a service provider should be explained by that provider and handled in accordance with applicable law.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Calls may be routed or tracked using third-party telecommunications services to help handle inquiries, measure referral activity, or connect callers with relevant providers. The precise routing and tracking arrangements depend on the telephone service configured for this website and should be confirmed by the operator.

This policy does not state that calls are recorded. If call recording is introduced or used, callers must receive appropriate notice and any consent required by applicable law before recording takes place.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The page supplied for this website does not include an identified analytics service, advertising pixel, or cookie-consent tool. Basic cookies or similar technologies may nevertheless be used by the hosting platform or other technical services, depending on their configuration. The operator should check the live website and hosting settings before making any definitive statement about cookies or analytics.

7. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with an independent roofing service provider when this is needed to handle or refer your inquiry. It may also be processed by technical or telecommunications providers involved in hosting the website, operating the telephone service, or maintaining the site's functionality. Those providers should only receive information relevant to the services they perform or as otherwise permitted by law.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users, or the rights of others. This policy does not mean that information is never shared; referrals to independent providers are part of the website's lead-generation purpose.

8. Data Security and Retention

The website operator should take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including handling inquiries, managing referrals, maintaining appropriate business records, and meeting legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the systems and service providers used.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have the right to ask for access to personal information, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion or restriction of certain information. You may also have other rights under applicable law.

To make a privacy request, contact the website operator using the contact details that should be added to this policy before the site is published. If your information has already been passed to an independent service provider, you may also need to contact that provider directly about information it holds.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

This website may refer visitors to independent service providers or external websites. Those businesses and websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies, systems, and practices. The website operator is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third parties. Please review their privacy information before providing them with personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, its services, or its information-handling practices change. The current version will be published on this page. Please review it periodically to see whether any changes have been made.

Before publication: The website operator should add a working privacy contact method and verify the live hosting logs, telephone routing or tracking arrangements, any call-recording settings, and any cookies or analytics enabled by the hosting environment or third-party services. This policy is a general description and does not, by itself, establish compliance with every privacy law that may apply.
